1

Farm Location & Pinning

2

Live Weather & Day/Night

Real-time Open-Meteo satellite feed with interactive Day/Night simulation, 7-day precipitation forecasts, and 5-year historical climate averages.

3

Soil Classification AI

Fine-tuned MobileNetV2 pedological classifier with non-soil rejection gates, ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 API, and Soil Health Card NPK calibration.
